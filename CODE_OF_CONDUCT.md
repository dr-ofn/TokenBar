# Code of Conduct

We want TokenBar to be a friendly, productive place for everyone who files an issue, sends a patch or asks a question.

## Our standard

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Keep feedback specific and constructive.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing or sexualized language.
- Do not post other people's private data or your own secrets (API keys, tokens, personal log contents) in issues. Redact them first.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, close threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, open an issue titled "Conduct" without private details, or contact a maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile.

This text is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
